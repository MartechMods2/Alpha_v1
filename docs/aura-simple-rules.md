✨ *AURA — SIMPLE MEMBER GUIDE*

Aura is Alpha's virtual currency. It is not real money and cannot be cashed out. Only the verified owner has unlimited Aura.

*CHECK & EARN*
• $aura — your wallet and profile.
• $aura daily — 500 Aura once daily. Every 7th streak claim adds 2,000.
• $aura quests 1 — answer a quiz; use $aura answer 1 2 for option 2. Two quizzes daily; each correct answer earns 750 Aura.
• $aura redeem CODE — claim a shared code. Drops: 8am, 2pm and 8pm Nigerian time, when Alpha is online. First valid claim wins 20,000; one scheduled win per person daily.

*SHOP & SHARE*
• $aura shop — see items and current prices.
• $aura shop buy byte-fox — buy an item. Purchases of 100,000+ need --confirm.
• $aura shop equip ruby-frame — equip an owned cosmetic.
• $aura inventory — your items. Bags start with 20 slots; $aura bag shows upgrades. Cards and earned prestige rewards use no slots.
• $aura send @member 1000 — preview a transfer; add --confirm to send. Fee: 5%, rounded up. Maximum: 10,000 per transfer and 20,000 sent daily.
• $aura shop gift @member byte-fox — preview gifting ONE owned item; add --confirm to send. No fee; ten item gifts daily. Recipient needs space and required XP/Evo rank. Gifts are final.
• $aura thanks @member — thank three different people daily. Gift to five different members for Community Giver; receive thanks from ten different members for Community Favourite. Badges give no Aura or XP.

*RANKS & RULES*
• $aura evo — see permanent Evo ranks, prices and quest requirements. Evo and Evo-Legendary+ award exclusive frames and titles. Special storage is bought separately.
• Spending Aura does not reduce lifetime XP. Bought ranks never grant admin powers. Earned ranks, prestige frames, badges and cards cannot be gifted.
• Do not spam, scam or farm rewards. No Aura fine for silence, wrong quiz answers or normal game losses. Only the owner reviews penalties; mistaken penalties can be appealed.
• Read previews before confirming. Failed paid features refund their Aura fee. $aura history sends your records privately.
• When Aura is OFF, earning and spending pause; your saved balance and items stay.
